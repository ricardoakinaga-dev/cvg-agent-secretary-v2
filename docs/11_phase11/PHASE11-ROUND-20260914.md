## Release boundary

The local evidence proves only the synthetic controlled slice. It does not
validate real providers, channels, external identity, institutional RAG,
physical durability, RPO/RTO, pilot behavior, or human release sign-off.
Those gates remain `NOT_VALIDATED` or `PENDING`, and production remains
`NO-GO`.

The machine-readable current result and manifest are generated under
`certification/phase11-result.json` and
`certification/phase11-manifest.json`; `npm run certification:verify:phase11`
must pass against the unchanged candidate after the final certification run.
